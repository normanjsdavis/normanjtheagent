# Real estate use (normanjtheagent: Lake Murray & Columbia, SC)

Local add-on to Samin Yasar's official motion-ads skill. Read this whenever the subject is a property, listing, market update or agent brand.

- **Listing promo (Level 1, free):** listing photos, logo and brand kit. An 8 to 12 shot
  storyboard: exterior hero, price/beds/baths, key rooms, neighborhood/lake, agent
  card/CTA. Slide-up text, center framing, transforms between rooms.
- **Market-update motion graphic:** animated charts (median price, days on market,
  inventory) with the numbers sourced and dated on screen.
- **Lifestyle reel (Level 2):** real lake/community footage, sorted and cut to 9:16 with a VO.
- **Compliance (always):** fair-housing-safe language (describe the property, never the
  "ideal buyer"); brokerage name on screen per SC advertising rules; AI-enhanced or virtually
  staged visuals labeled as such; no altered views, no hidden defects; exact figures only
  from the MLS/listing data.

## Level choice for property work
- **Level 1 (code):** market-update charts, just-listed/just-sold cards, price/beds/baths reveals, agent end card. Free.
- **Level 2 (footage):** real walkthrough or drone footage cut into a 15 to 30 s promo with slide-up text and an AI voiceover.
- **Level 3 (photoreal):** twilight or hero-shot motion from real listing photos (keyframes from the real photos only; never change the architecture, the view or the condition).

## Honesty (works with references/honesty.md)
- Label AI-enhanced or virtually staged frames on screen or in the caption.
- Use exact numbers only from the MLS/listing; show the source and date on market charts.
