VA loan basics

You earned this benefit. Here is what it does.

  • Many eligible buyers put no money down.
  • No monthly mortgage insurance.
  • A one-time VA funding fee applies to most buyers. Veterans who receive VA disability compensation are generally exempt.
  • The VA appraisal checks that the home meets the VA's minimum property requirements.
  • You need a Certificate of Eligibility. Your lender can usually pull it for you.

I am a REALTOR®, not a lender. Your lender confirms your eligibility, rates, and costs.

Questions military families ask me

Do you help military families moving to Fort Jackson?

Yes. I am a Military Relocation Professional and I still serve in the Army Reserve. I help families moving to Fort Jackson, Shaw Air Force Base, and McEntire Joint National Guard Base buy and sell homes across the full Columbia-area MLS.

Do you help buyers in Northeast Columbia, Blythewood, or Lake Carolina near Fort Jackson?

Yes. My home base is Lexington and Lake Murray, but I work the whole Columbia-area MLS. I travel to show homes and close deals in Northeast Columbia, Lake Carolina, Blythewood, Forest Acres, Elgin, and the surrounding area.

Can I buy a home before I arrive in South Carolina?

Yes. Many military buyers tour homes by live video, get pre-approved from where they are now, and make an offer before they report. We build the plan around your report date.

Do I need a down payment with a VA loan?

Many eligible buyers use a VA loan with no down payment and no monthly mortgage insurance. Your lender confirms what you qualify for. I can connect you with lenders who handle VA loans in the Midlands.

Do I have to pay the VA funding fee?

Most VA buyers pay a one-time funding fee, and it can usually be rolled into the loan. Veterans who receive VA disability compensation are generally exempt. Your lender confirms your status on your Certificate of Eligibility.

Can I use a VA loan in Lexington, Blythewood, or on Lake Murray?

Yes. A VA loan works on any home that will be your primary residence and meets the VA's minimum property requirements. The VA appraisal checks those requirements. I walk you through what that means for the homes you like before you make an offer.

What is a Military Relocation Professional (MRP)?

MRP is a certification from the National Association of REALTORS® for agents trained in military moves, PCS timelines, and VA financing.

Should I buy or rent when I PCS?

It depends on how long you expect to stay, your monthly budget, and what you want to do with the home if you get new orders. My free Buyer Game Plan has a buy now or wait worksheet. We can also run the numbers together.
