Privacy Policy — Hermes Personal

This application is a personal productivity tool used solely by its owner, Norman J. Davis, to read his own Gmail accounts.

What it accesses

Read-only access to the Gmail inbox of accounts explicitly authorized by the owner. The app does not send, delete, modify, or share any email.

Who uses it

This app has no public users. It is authorized only for a small number of email accounts personally owned or controlled by Norman J. Davis.

Data sharing

No data is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party. Email content is processed locally and used only to generate a private daily summary for the owner.
